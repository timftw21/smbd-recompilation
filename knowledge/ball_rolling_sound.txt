Recordings
The player table at 581620 selects sound-table types 52..54. The sound table
at 579960 maps them to effects 248, 273 and 274, which allse.scp assigns to
one-based allse.acx entries 111, 2 and 118. All are mono 4-bit ADX v4 at
44100 Hz with a 500 Hz predictor cutoff.

Name            Type  Effect  ACX  Samples  Content
SND_SE01_RUN01  0x52    248   111     6393  steady hiss, level throughout
SND_SE01_RUN02  0x53    273     2    12682  rumble swell: 10 ms fade-in from
                                            silence, steady to 190 ms, decays
                                            back to silence by 288 ms
SND_SE01_RUN03  0x54    274   118    17584  clatter, tapering to silence at
                                            both ends

All 183 allse.acx entries carry a whole-file ADX loop and every allse.scp
line uses playback mode 0, so neither file says which effects loop. The ADX
data above decodes bit-identically to an independent ADX decoder. The
GameCube dumps of RUN01..03 (GMBE8P-AudioManager misc_sfx.rpp) have the same
full lengths, 0.145, 0.288 and 0.399 s. Looped whole with the decoder's
history carried across the seam, each joins continuously: the step from the
last to the first sample is 1599, 0 and 3, against 99.9th-percentile steps
of 14892, 2957 and 2796 within the recordings.

Original controller
GameCube Super Monkey Ball mixes the same three effects in func_8002CA5C
(sound.c, smb-decomp revision 5cd6ffccf3f8508231c7cd9cce5b722be4465a2b,
https://github.com/camthesaxman/smb-decomp). The ball update calls it every
eighth unpaused frame, or at once with zero speed when contact is lost.
On contact it starts all three voices; each update sets volume (ctrl 7) and
MusyX doppler (ctrl 0x84, sndFXCtrl14) on all three; at zero speed or volume
it keys them off. ctrl 0x80 is the pitch wheel (SoundPitch) and 0x84 is
doppler (SoundDop). SMB1's own MusyX routes ctrl 0x84 to the doppler input
(snd_midictrl.s 800FF018) and scales the voice pitch by it at synth.s
800ECBC8: pitch=(pitch>>16)*doppler>>13, so 8192 keeps the recorded rate.
Playback runs at 44100 * doppler / 8192 Hz. An octave-lower base was tried
and rejected by ear against the GameCube games.

Deluxe E9180 is the same controller. 8B907..8B9BA computes speed
min((km/h-10)*1.5,127) and volume min((km/h-10)*15,80)*0.85 like the GameCube
caller. Unlike the GameCube, E91E9..E9216 multiplies the volume by 1.5
(clamped to 127). Layer curves are 2-point Hermite curves evaluated by ADBB0
(t*t*(3-2t) with zero tangents):
  5816E0 RUN01 volume 0 -> 127
  581700 RUN02 volume 127 -> 127 (constant)
  581720 RUN03 volume 0 -> 127
  58174C doppler 4608 -> 15615 (0.5625x .. 1.906x)
Each layer volume is (boosted volume / 127) * curve(speed). Deluxe evaluates
the doppler curve at E9422 and discards it at E9427. Its control shim E3C60
honours only ctrl 7; pan and doppler are dropped. E53F7 rejects effect 273,
and with mode 0 the other two recordings play once. So retail Deluxe only
plays one-shots of RUN01 and RUN03 when rolling starts.

Volume law: E2610 maps a Deluxe volume v to -100*log10(127/v) hundredths of
a decibel, an amplitude of sqrt(v/127). MusyX's default volume table
(hw_volconv.c musyx_vol_tab, voice volTable 0) is close to (v/127)^2.15 (within
0.6 dB from 16 to 127). At the GameCube's maximum rolling volume of 68 the
constant RUN02 layer is -12 dB in MusyX but -2.7 dB with E2610 and Deluxe's
1.5x boost.

Panning: CRI's driver (2CEE40, table 45E160) sends a centred mono effect to
the centre mix bin at full volume, which the mixer folds to -3 dB per side.
MusyX's centred pan is the same -3 dB (pan_tab 0.7079).

Native restoration
The setting is latched per player while a roll is active (765708 flag).
- E9180 entry reads the player, speed and volume. If the setting changed
  during a roll, E921A jumps to the stop path at E9685 first.
- E934A: instead of E53F0, start a native looping voice for the layer with
  the controller's volume (BL) and return handle -1. E3C60 and E34A0 ignore
  -1, so the original controller state stays consistent.
- E95E8: each update passes the final layer volume (including multiplayer
  scaling) to the native voice.
- E9685: the stop path releases the native voices.
- Doppler is evaluated natively with the original 58174C curve on start and
  every update, and applied to all three layers.
- Volume: the Deluxe layer volume is scaled back by the request/boosted
  ratio to the GameCube value, then mapped with (v/127)^2.15.
- Loops: all three recordings loop whole at their full lengths. Each is
  decoded twice so the first block starts from the predictor history at
  the end of the file, as a looping ADX decoder continues. An earlier
  version looped only RUN02's steady 40..190 ms and crossfaded 10 ms off
  RUN01 and RUN03; its 135..150 ms cycles repeated noticeably too fast.
- Level changes ramp over 5 ms (one MusyX DSP frame). Key-off fades over
  30 ms; the GameCube macro's release length is not on the disc.
- A frame tick stops a roll with the game's own zero-speed request if E9180
  has not refreshed it for 12 ticks, during the Story pause (892114 mask 0xA)
  or after the setting is turned off, and releases voices whose 765708 flag
  was cleared without a stop.

Validation
smbd-tool check-rolling-sound XBE ISO OUTPUT verifies the tables and
recordings, measures each loop seam and renders a scripted roll (rest,
acceleration to 100 km/h, a bounce, deceleration, loss of contact) to
classic-rolling.wav with peak, clipping, silence and level checks. Seam
level changes are reported but do not gate the result, because RUN02's
swell and RUN03's taper are part of the recordings.
