FMV subtitle audit and corrections, 2026-11-16

Executable SHA-256:
9B49918BC4CD0D2541369534F9A458679F43C63462869F0F37C0ED69B3349DC2

Scope. Story and ending cutscenes draw subtitles from media/dlg/*_e.bin,
loaded by path beside scnplay/scndat_*.bin. The executable names 23 English
scripts: scn1a, scn1b, scn2..scn10, scnlast, scnend1..4 and scnend1u..6u
(433 entries). scn0a_e.bin has no reference. scnall appears only in the name
table at 38EE24 beside the DIALOG/SCENE PLAY TEST strings and is not loaded
by path. The attract/opening movies have no subtitle scripts.

Format. Loader 6B26F opens the file, sizes it (16A70), allocates
(size+1F)&~1F plus 20h and reads it whole (16A00). Header: u32 entry count,
u32 table offset (8), one offset per 64-byte entry. 6B2E5..6B2F6 relocate the
table, entry +00 (cue label string, e.g. "214") and +2C (text) by the buffer
base. Labels are stored directly after each text's terminator. Text codes:
\tTIME reveal/pause marks (10000+TIME closes the card), \pN styles, \l page
breaks, LF line breaks. Renderer 6B900 treats a byte with its high bit set as
the lead of a two-byte character (6B994) and passes both bytes on (74810).

Glyph lookup 73D70, ASCII mode, maps A-Z/a-z/0-9 arithmetically and anything
else through table 39E6D8 indexed by the signed byte. Byte 84 reads 39E4E8
(bytes 53 41 4E 4E, part of an unrelated string) and D6 reads 39E630
(FF 12 FF FF); "!" (21) reads glyph 133h. Six English lines still end in the
Japanese heart code 84 D6, used throughout the Japanese scripts; the
parallel English lines end in "!".

Corrections (Enhancements / Corrected subtitles, setting corrected_subtitles,
default on). src/subtitles.cpp holds 32 splice edits and 10 label-pointer
updates for 15 scripts, keyed by each script's size and SHA-256. They apply
only to a complete read at offset 0 of an unmodified original, on the
synchronous, uncached asynchronous and cached asynchronous read paths; the
read cache keeps original bytes. A text that grows moves the label after it
into its own zero padding and updates the entry's +00 pointer; file size,
entry count, labels and all timing/style codes stay identical.
  - 84 D6 -> "!" (scnend1u 23, scnend2u 3/23, scnend3u 27, scnend5u 6,
    scnend6u 1).
  - "hapiness" -> "happiness" in the shared closing chant (scnend4, 4u, 5u, 6u).
  - "I know it's a bery hard" -> "I know it's bery hard" in all ten endings
    (Baby's "bery" kept; the stray article is not part of the speech pattern).
  - "a long way to become an / Ultimate player." -> "a long way to go /
    to become an Ultimate player." (scnend1u, scnend2u; same line count).
  - scnend3u 22 begins with a duplicated "pwayer! " from the previous card.
  - scnend3u 7 gains its final period.
  - scn1b "any more" -> "anymore"; scn3 "So long, everyone!" and
    "Hang on, everyone!"; scn4 "high-speed" -> "high speed"; scn5
    "Aaaah..., Oh" -> "Aaaah... Oh"; scn9 "forget, AIAI!", "onto the planet",
    "That means you'll".

Left as intended: Baby's and GonGon's speech (da, dis, bery, pway, tink,
weally, blowed, Wroagh), chants, double spaces after sentences, padded short
cards such as "  Gasp!  ", cards that continue a sentence, "Here, I go!" and
"strange ..." (their punctuation sits on timed pauses), colloquial "You
better" and spoken comma splices.

Validation: smbd-tool check-subtitles ISO passes 840 checks (34 changed and
326 unchanged entries; labels, codes and English-only bytes verified; partial,
modified and non-dlg reads are left unchanged). check-services passes 232
checks, including scn9 reads on all three delivery paths with the option on
and off. Captured cutscenes were not replayed in-game for these scenes.
