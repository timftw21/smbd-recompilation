Frontend texture edges at higher resolutions (2026-11-17)
XBE SHA-256: 9B49918BC4CD0D2541369534F9A458679F43C63462869F0F37C0ED69B3349DC2

Symptom
Thin dark vertical/horizontal lines beside HUD and menu text at internal
resolutions above 1x, with or without Upscaled HUD and antialiasing.
User capture frame-1794875095213-113965613400 (3x, 16:9, MSAA 4x, 1-14
SLOPES goal): lines at the edges of "1-14", "66mph", "STAGE SCORE",
"TIME BONUS" and "REPLAY" glyph quads, through their drop shadows.

Cause (executable and capture evidence)
- Every frontend sprite and glyph is one quad from 31200..31462, completed
  at 313B0 (knowledge/ultrawide_hud.txt). Font digits share atlases: draw
  372 ("1") samples texture 50 (128x64 DXT3) at u 0.125..0.25, v 1/64..1/4,
  one 16-texel cell. The neighbouring "2" cell's outline starts at column
  32, the very first texel past this cell (decoded alpha 6..F).
- The SDK screen offset k59=(320.53125,240.53125), 1/16-pixel truncation
  and 1/32 bias place pixel centres so that, in these draws, the original
  640x480 raster never samples the neighbouring cell's first texel. In 16:9 the HUD is squeezed by
  0.75 (k96 x scale 0.00234375): 12 pixels cover 16 texels and the last
  centre samples texel 15.33 of the cell.
- At 3x, host pixel centres lie up to 1/3 pixel beyond the last original
  centre: the last samples texel 15.78, giving the neighbour's black
  outline 28% weight. Higher resolution, not the HUD upscaler or MSAA,
  adds these samples; replays with Upscaled HUD 0/1 and AA off/MSAA 4x
  all show the lines.

Fix
- complete_screen_primitives (src/cpu.cpp) reports, for every frontend quad
  whose UVs form a texture-space rectangle (any screen rotation), that
  rectangle per UV set. The report is keyed by the quad's vertex bytes
  (interface_quad_identity); 313B0 submits those bytes unchanged as the
  NV097 inline array, so Gpu::draw matches each quad of an inline QUADS draw.
  Draws hold one quad or one cell repeated at sub-pixel offsets (outline and
  shadow passes); all quads must report the same rectangle. Across 18
  captures, all 2,513 frontend draws were one quad or four copies of one UV
  rectangle. Unmatched draws keep ordinary sampling.
- Gpu::draw insets the rectangle by half a texel of the stage's level-0 size
  (from the NV097 format word) and clamps only plain 2D lookups of swizzled
  textures. Bilinear sampling of the full-resolution level then reads only
  the quad's own texels; samples farther inside are unchanged. Linear
  textures and surface copies use texel coordinates and are never clamped.
- The combiner and modulate2d pixel shaders apply the clamp above 1x only;
  1x keeps the original sampling.
- Captures record "bounded_samples"/"sample_bounds" per draw; replay-frame
  restores them, and --sample-bounds 0 replays without the clamp.

Rejected first attempt (same day)
Clamping to the coordinates of the first and last 640x480 pixel centres
removed the lines but moved edge samples up to a full texel inward on
minified glyphs (STAGE SCORE letters: 24 texels over 12.6 pixels in 16:9),
smearing outlines into heavier black borders. It also mapped normalized
bounds onto Mode Select's scene-copy quad (draw 182, linear texture 74 with
texel coordinates), pinning the 3D background to one sky-blue texel.
Capture frame-1794877053413-92750787300 shows that regression.

Refinement: digit tops and edges cut off (2026-11-20)
Symptom: user captures frame-1795194721984-203444685500 and
frame-1795198287348-117168830400 (3x, 16:9, MSAA 4x, Upscaled HUD): the top
of the blue lives "2" and of the white "0" in "0mph" is flat. Upscaled HUD
on or off does not matter.
Cause: three digit atlases address a regular cell without its first texel
line, while their art starts on it. Lives digits (128x128 DXT3): v 1..24 and
49..72 of 24-row cells; speed digits (128x64): v 1..16, 17..32, 33..48 of
16-row cells; timer digits (256x128): u 1..28, 29..56, 57..84 of 28-column
cells. Decoded alpha shows the top outline on row 0 (lives "2": columns
44..53) and the timer digits' left outline on column 0/28/56. The original
raster's first pixel centre lies 1/32 pixel inside the rasterized leading
edge (v = 1.03 texels), so its first row blends that line at about 47% over
a whole pixel, half of it outside the quad. Above 1x only the geometry is
painted, so the line shrinks to a faint sliver; the 11-17 half-texel clamp
(v >= 1.5) removed it entirely.
Rule:
- complete_screen_primitives reports, for screen-aligned quads whose UV
  components each follow one axis, the UVs at the original's outermost
  covered pixel centres, which UV component follows which screen axis and
  leads high, and per axis the vertex coordinate that rasterizes at the
  boundary of the first covered pixel (k-1/32). Guest vertices are unchanged.
- Gpu::draw keeps the half-texel inset and widens each bound only to the
  original's outermost samples, so no texel gets more weight than the
  original's edge pixels gave it.
- When a rectangle is a regular cell without its first line (far edge on a
  cell boundary, leading edge one texel past the previous one), the leading
  bound reaches that line's centre and the draw's private vertex copy moves
  the leading edge to the first pixel boundary, extending the UVs along the
  axis. 1x coverage and every original sample stay the same; above 1x the
  band the original's first row painted shows the line's art.
Rejected (same day): moving every frontend quad to the original's pixel
coverage. Fonts whose UVs sit on cell boundaries (the 256x128 16x16-cell
score font) then showed the neighbouring cell's last line as a hard band and
a dark sliver at their top and left edges, or a flat grey lid when clamped to
the first centre's UV. Only the three atlases above match the cell rule
across 1,694 bounded UV edges in seven captures.
Validation (RTX 5060 Ti; scratch routes: Challenge Beginner 1 PLAIN in play
and at the goal, at the user's settings): the lives "2", speed "0" and timer
digits regain their outlines; the goal screen's score, STAGE SCORE, TIME
BONUS and "X 2" render as before. check-hud-upscaling, check-cpu and
check-image-copy pass; a replay of the new capture differs from it in 273
pixels, as replays do. Captures made before this change replay with their
recorded vertices and bounds.

World 6 camera steam at higher internal resolutions (2026-11-24)

The two Windows captures show 6-3 Punched Seesaws in widescreen on an RTX
5060 Ti. Frame 12317 at 1x draws the full-screen steam at draw 436. Frame
13637 at 3x draws it at draw 435. Both use the same 256x256 texture at
01BF1000, additive blending and wrapping on both texture axes.

The captured vertex program matches all nine instructions in the verified
US disc's media/Shaders/vs/fmt0x2a00/Flag0x00001000.xvu. It uses DPH on
the vertex UVs with constant rows 127 and 128. At 3x those rows translate
the lookup by (4.23187, 7.40160). Clamping the translated lookup to the
quad's original 0..1 rectangle samples one corner across the screen.
The remaining vertical gradient comes from the quad's vertex alpha.
The published 0.7.5 and 0.8.0 tools produced identical faulty overlays.

Live submission and replay now check the frontend texture matrix per UV set
before using input-space atlas bounds. The second set uses rows 130 and
131. Identity transforms keep the atlas edge handling for HUD text.
Replaying the same steam draw at 1x, 2x, 3x and 4x matched ordinary
wrapped sampling in every RGB pixel. The full corrected 627-draw frame
also matched an independent replay with only draw 435's clamp disabled.

A fresh muted Windows run selected World 6, stage 3 through the Practice
menu using a private save copy. Its 3x capture contains 632 draws. The
original steam shader ran on draw 436 with both axes wrapping and no atlas
bounds. The 108 HUD draws still used atlas bounds. All 38 Windows HUD
diagnostic cases passed. The full 1x frame differed from the prior replay
at two pixels by one RGB level. Repeated runs of the old tool also varied
by one level. The isolated 3x overlay matched its ordinary-sampling control
on Ubuntu 24.04 under WSL2 with llvmpipe.
